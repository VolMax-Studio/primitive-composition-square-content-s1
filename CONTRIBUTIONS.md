# Contributions

## Origin

The question arose from Ivan Nestorov's reflection on an image about numerals
and geometry in September 2026 (provenance summarized in `ORIGIN.md`). This
led to the idea of viewing primitive Pythagorean triples through Gaussian
integers ("angles") and asking when their composition loses primitivity.

## Direction and responsibility

Ivan Nestorov (VolMax Studio Lab) selected the problem, directed the
investigation, evaluated the resulting mathematical claims, and performed the
final human ratification of the formal artifacts.

## AI assistance

Several AI systems were used under Ivan Nestorov's direction for mathematical
exploration, proof development, counterexample searches, Lean 4 formalization,
and adversarial review.

Claude (Anthropic, Opus) performed an AI gate review of mediated artifacts. It
did not have direct repository access and did not independently reproduce the
Lean kernel build.

No AI system is listed as an author.

## Novelty

Mathematical novelty is not claimed.

Rung A is assessed as likely folklore. For Rung B, no explicit formulation was
identified in the reviewed literature, but the result appears to be a relatively
direct consequence of standard facts about Gaussian integers. This assessment
is intentionally conservative and does not constitute a claim of priority.

The contribution claimed here is the explicit formulation, machine-checked
Lean 4 proof artifacts, reproducible verification package, and associated
provenance record.
