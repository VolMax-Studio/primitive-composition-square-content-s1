# Origin

Date: 2026-09-22

This project began from a question posed by Ivan Nestorov while reflecting on
an image about numerals, geometry, and the possibility of representing
arithmetic structure geometrically.

The original prompt asked, in exploratory terms, whether relationships between
Pythagorean triples, geometric angles, and number composition could expose
simpler mathematical structure.

That question led to the subsequent investigation of primitive Pythagorean
triples through Gaussian-integer multiplication and, eventually, to the
specific gcd and square-content statements formalized in this repository.

The initial prompt did not state the final theorems. The mathematical claims,
counterexample tests, proofs, and Lean formalization were developed afterward.

This note records the provenance of the research question only. It is not a
claim of mathematical novelty or priority.
